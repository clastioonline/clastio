# Read-only published policy content audit — 12 October 2026

Scope: public localhost:3005 legal API responses and repository source inspection. No published documents, account records, billing settings or user content were changed. No account deletion, refund, export of a real teacher's data, paid generation or new broad test suite was executed. This is a content/feature coverage review, not a jurisdictional legal-compliance determination.

## Current public state

Five current documents return HTTP 200: terms, privacy, acceptable_use, cookie and refund. Each is published version 1.0, effective/published on 26 September 2026, with one public history entry. Terms/privacy/acceptable_use require acceptance; cookie/refund do not. Copyright/DMCA is a supported document type but `/api/v1/legal/dmca` returns 404. Draft/unpublished database counts were not queried. See `published-summary.json`.

All five published bodies retain a PPT Genie template-review notice. Terms and acceptable-use text retain the former product name. Privacy includes `privacy@pptgenie.example (replace with your real contact)`. The current source defaults say Clastio and contain newer cookie wording, but they do not update existing published documents.

Evidence: `apps/api/app/services/legal.py:49` removes only the exact current notice prefix; `:98` defines the Clastio notice; `:185` seeds a type only when no current published document exists. `apps/api/app/seed.py:114` invokes that seed. Published versions are immutable at `apps/api/app/api/routes/admin.py:1072`; reviewed replacements require a new version rather than changing historical consent text.

## Findings requiring resolution before relying on the current text

1. **Operator/contact details are absent.** No registered operator name, business address, registration details, working privacy/copyright contact or dispute/governing-law choice appears in these five short published templates. The placeholder contact cannot support real privacy requests. These are business/legal decisions to supply and review; this audit does not invent them.

2. **Cookie inventory is outdated.** The published policy mentions only session and preference cookies. The app now also sets the signed random `clastio_installation` browser cookie for up to one year and stores a hashed claim to limit repeated trials/rewards. Evidence: `apps/api/app/services/trial_device.py:14`, `:15`, `:31`, `:43`. Cookie choices themselves are stored in localStorage, not a preference cookie: `apps/web/components/cookie-banner.tsx:8`, `:18`, `:21`. The newer source policy describes the installation cookie at `apps/api/app/services/legal.py:171`, but that wording is not in the current public response. Optional analytics choices do not establish that an analytics vendor is currently enabled.

3. **AI/provider disclosure is incomplete and contains an unverified blanket promise.** Public privacy lists Anthropic/OpenAI/Google, while the actual adapter sends requests through OpenRouter and can route search to Perplexity. Evidence: `apps/api/app/ai/openrouter_provider.py:18`, `:40`. Its request settings at `:52` require supported parameters but do not specify no-training/zero-retention routing. The repository cannot establish the published claim that all providers do not train under the operator's agreements. Actual account settings/provider contracts and enabled hosting, email, identity, payment and monitoring vendors need verification; no conclusion that a particular provider trains on this app's data is made here. Public/source claim: `apps/api/app/services/legal.py:147`.

4. **Physical deletion of generated content is incomplete in source.** Public privacy promises content removal after a 30-day grace period. `apps/api/app/services/lifecycle.py:83` deletes uploaded originals and educator exports (`:96`, `:101`) before deleting owned database rows. No equivalent cleanup is present for normal lesson PPT/PDF/all-version previews, document export files, custom template bases/previews, assets or media blobs. Their storage writes are visible at `apps/api/app/services/courses.py:631`–`:649`, `apps/api/app/services/documents.py:152`, `:210`–`:234`, `apps/api/app/services/styles.py:380`–`:381`, and `apps/api/app/services/media.py:208`–`:209`. No orphan/prefix cleanup was found in the inspected storage/service code. This is a source-inspection finding, not an executed deletion test or proof of public access to deleted content. Account access is revoked immediately and owned database rows are removed; inaccessible retained blobs still exceed the blanket physical-removal wording. Storage-delete exceptions also allow purge completion (`lifecycle.py:97`) without a durable storage retry demonstrated here.

5. **The Settings data export is partial.** `apps/api/app/api/routes/teacher.py:124`–`:145` returns profile/preferences and summaries of classes/courses/lessons/documents/uploads plus an AI-call count. It does not export actual slide specifications, full document content, conversations, teacher memory, generated files, consent history, billing or security data. Public privacy's general “export your data” sentence should describe this scope or the export should be expanded. No real teacher export was requested in this audit.

6. **Retention wording omits retained categories and configuration.** Default request/security retention matches the advertised 90 days/one year. The scheduler also uses analytics 400 days, read notifications 180 days and completed email outbox 90 days; admins can configure those periods (`apps/api/app/services/lifecycle.py:146`). After deletion, billing, consent, audit, security and some other evidence remain (`:40`), with personal fields selectively cleared (`:106`). The public paragraph does not explain these categories, processor/backup retention, a defined accounting/evidence period, or how to request deletion cancellation during the grace period. Actual backup/provider retention and deployed scheduler operation were not verified here.

7. **Sharing and intellectual-property scope need clearer coverage.** Terms permit processing “only to provide the service to you” (`apps/api/app/services/legal.py:116`), but the app supports organisation-shared templates (`apps/api/app/api/routes/content.py:365`) and staff-published shared books that other teachers can copy (`:240`, `:278`). Publication requires a licence/reference URL and explicit permission to share with all teachers (`:291`), which is a useful control; the policy should explain that separate sharing scope, copied material, publisher/stock licences and AI-output rights without implying that every referenced textbook PDF is licensed or imported. Generic copyright prohibition exists; a practical public copyright complaint contact/procedure is absent. No universal ownership/legal-rights conclusion about AI output is made.

8. **Refund promises need an operational, owner-approved process.** Public policy promises annual pro-rata and wholly unused media-pack refunds within 14 days, but provides only “contact support” and no functioning contact, processing timeline, evidence required or calculation. The code has checkout/portal/cancellation and generation-credit restoration, not an in-app monetary refund request/approval implementation (`apps/api/app/services/billing.py:180`, `:285`, `:466`; `apps/api/app/services/media.py:224`). Monetary refunds may be handled manually by the payment partner; that process was not verified and is not claimed absent. Clarify money refunds versus returned generation credits, currencies/taxes, non-renewing/manual licences, trial choice and statutory exceptions after appropriate review.

9. **Collected-data/vendor detail is too short for the features now present.** The app additionally has assistant conversations, voice/media prompts, teaching memory, class/timetable history, support requests, device-push endpoints, WhatsApp contacts/messages and hashed anti-abuse claims. The broad “content/usage” headings do not explain these specific uses or recipients. Signed payment webhook payloads are stored (`apps/api/app/services/billing.py:866`), so “only plan, amounts and invoice references” understates payment-related metadata; the audit did not inspect private payloads or assert that full card numbers are stored. Privacy says do not upload student data, while acceptable use permits personal data with a lawful basis; decide and express one clear product rule.

## Controls that do exist

Current versions/history and effective dates are returned by the public API (`apps/api/app/api/routes/account.py:28`). Signup records the current terms/privacy/acceptable-use versions (`apps/api/app/api/routes/auth.py:130`). Marketing consent is separately changeable (`apps/api/app/api/routes/account.py:85`). A new document marked `requires_acceptance` prompts the browser to review and accept it (`apps/web/components/legal-gate.tsx:12`); that setting is important for revised documents. Account deletion locks/revokes sessions immediately and schedules a 30-day purge (`apps/api/app/services/lifecycle.py:45`). Paid cancellations are set for period end (`apps/api/app/services/billing.py:466`). These controls do not resolve the content and storage-cleanup gaps above.
