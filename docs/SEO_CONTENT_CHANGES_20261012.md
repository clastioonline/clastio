## Primary sources checked before content edits

Google’s advice informed the research and editorial method; these SEO documents are not inserted into the teaching guides as irrelevant reader references.

- [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): focus on readers, originality and honest authorship.
- [Google’s guidance on generative AI content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content): review generated content and metadata for accuracy; avoid producing pages without added value.
- [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): avoid scaled-content and doorway-page practices.

The reader-facing guides link the relevant original sources:

- [Department for Education: National curriculum](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-curriculum): programmes of study for England; the guide asks teachers which programme their school follows.
- [Department for Education: GCSE subject content](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/gcse-subject-content): subject-content reference, with the actual board specification still supplied by the teacher.
- [Cambridge IGCSE overview](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-upper-secondary/cambridge-igcse/): qualification context; aligned standards do not identify a class’s exact syllabus.
- [Cambridge IGCSE Physics 0625](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-physics-0625/): example subject reference; the syllabus year is the examination year.
- [Microsoft: supported PowerPoint formats](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/powerpoint/file-formats-that-are-supported-in-powerpoint): PPTX can also contain picture-based slides, so the guide requires an object-edit test.
- [British Council: AI use in teaching](https://africa.teachingenglish.org.uk/skills/tips/ai-safe-responsible-use): review outputs and avoid identifiable learner data.
- [Clastio pricing](/pricing): live plan details; all credit rates in the worked calculation are expressly labelled assumptions.

No examination questions or proprietary textbook content were copied into the public pages. Prompt examples and comparison criteria are original workflow instructions. New search intents are research hypotheses, not promised traffic or rankings.
