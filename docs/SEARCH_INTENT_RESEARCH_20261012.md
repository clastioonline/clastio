# Teacher search-intent research — 12 October 2026

## Scope and method

Priority confirmed by the owner: **UAE teachers, especially British-curriculum teachers**. CBSE/NCERT secondary teaching is a secondary opportunity, including Indian-curriculum schools in the UAE. A curriculum name does not determine a customer's country, regulator, or legal jurisdiction.

The 22 queries below were submitted to the available web search tool during this session. Evidence records selected returned page titles, primary publisher/vendor URLs, and what the result sample suggests about intent. These are **query hypotheses**, not measured popular keywords. Results were not obtained from a UAE-localised Google account; no UAE-only ranking, exact result position, search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, conversion rate, or AI-answer visibility data is available. Search results and vendor pages change. Third-party reviews appeared in some samples but are not treated as proof of another product's features, price, or quality.

Existing public `/solutions/…` guides, `/pricing`, `/faq`, `/how-it-works`, and `/for-schools` are the acquisition surfaces. Authenticated lesson editors, personal libraries, uploads, and teacher data are not keyword landing pages. The three selected additions below were implemented and checked locally after the research; no public deployment or search-traffic result is claimed. See the [implementation and validation record](SEARCH_GROWTH_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## Decisions for the first content pass

1. Lead with an editable teaching PowerPoint and the teacher's actual source material. Generic presentation results repeatedly expose PPTX export, editable objects, document inputs, and speaker notes. Demonstrate the resulting file and its boundaries instead of using an unsupported superlative.
2. Keep the UAE/British planning guide distinct from the presentation product guide: year group, subject, prior learning, school objectives, qualification/specification, and local classroom examples belong in planning guidance.
3. Give document-to-lesson searches a direct workflow: choose a relevant chapter or notes, review the plan, generate selected lessons, verify facts, edit/export. Do not imply permission to upload every commercial textbook or that source references are always verifiable.
4. Explain native editability, imported designs, and PDF limitations plainly. A `.pptx` filename alone does not establish that every element is editable. Show a download/edit check; do not promise arbitrary PDF object reconstruction.
5. Add a criteria-led buyer guide and a costs/credits guide. Distinguish a limited trial from a permanent free plan, and link to current pricing rather than freezing invented prices in articles.
6. Answer privacy and classroom-review questions with the actual policy and controls. Do not repeat vendor compliance labels or blanket no-training claims as evidence about Clastio.
7. Use GCSE/IGCSE and year/subject examples carefully. Several broad searches retrieved resources **about teaching AI**, rather than tools **using AI to make lessons**. Qualification, exam board, syllabus code, examination year, and tier are better discriminators than “British curriculum” alone.
8. Keep CBSE and EAL/differentiation supporting pages useful and focused. Do not create a page for every year/subject/city combination without a distinct example and workflow.

## Keyword vocabulary for measurement, not stuffing

Use these families to label real Search Console queries and understand teacher language. A term can belong to more than one family; match the complete query and landing page before deciding intent. Do not insert lists of synonyms into visible copy or create search-only doorway pages.

| Family | Useful variants | Ambiguities to examine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presentation output | PowerPoint, PPT, PPTX, lesson slides, lesson presentation, slide deck | University/business decks; tools producing page images only |
| Source workflow | textbook, chapter, PDF, teaching notes, lesson plan, upload, convert | Copyright/permission, scanned content, AI textbooks as a subject |
| UAE context | UAE, Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, British curriculum | Country mention does not show local ranking or regulator approval |
| Qualification | GCSE, IGCSE, KS3, KS4, Year 10, Cambridge, Edexcel, AQA | Distinct boards, syllabus versions, examination years and tiers |
| Editing/design | editable, text boxes, move images, edit a slide, school template, design reference | Arbitrary PDF editing; exact original-object recovery |
| Evaluation | choose, compare, alternative, best, versus, review | Vendor-written lists; “best” is subjective, not our measured result |
| Costs/access | price, pricing, credits, allowance, monthly, free, trial | Permanent free plan versus trial; token cost versus app credits |
| Trust/support | privacy, files, student data, source references, EAL, differentiation | Classroom guidance, AI literacy training, unverified compliance claims |
| CBSE secondary | CBSE, NCERT, Class 9, Class 10, science | India versus UAE schools; AI taught as a subject versus AI planning |

## Validation after publication

1. Confirm the chosen public page is crawlable, has the intended canonical, and says only what the reviewed product can demonstrate. Inspect mobile reading and the sample-file/editing workflow.
2. In Search Console, group actual queries by these intent families, inspect country/device/page, and record impressions, clicks and query language. Search-tool samples are not a ranking baseline.
3. Measure relevant page visits, pricing visits and trial starts without putting uploads, prompts, names, or classroom records into analytics events. Attribution and consent requirements belong to the implementation review.
4. Prioritise the pages earning relevant impressions and teacher trials. If a query mainly retrieves AI-subject materials or training, improve the wording or defer it; do not claim demand from a noisy sample.
5. For AI/search-answer acquisition, publish concise factual answers, supported examples, clear costs/limitations, and primary links. This sample establishes no inclusion in AI Overviews, chat answers, citations, or any future organic performance.
